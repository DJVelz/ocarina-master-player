Seeing all the hype for the new Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake made me want to learn how to play an ocarina.
I've been wanting to play one since I first played through Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask on my 3DS in high school.

After seeing how limited but dedicated the ocarina community is on platforms like Youtube and Reddit, I got the idea to make a practicing platform that suits my needs.

The point of Ocarina Master is to have three main features:

1. Sheet music with the correct fingering to be able to know the rhythm of songs and how to play the notes while I learn.
2. A highway mode similar to how apps like Yousician and Rocksmith to more intuitively see the timings of a song.
3. A visual song creator to easily create and share songs with the community.

You can visit the project at https://ocarina-master-player.vercel.app/
